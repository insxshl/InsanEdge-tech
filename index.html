GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few things businesses usually want to know before getting started.

Do you work only with businesses in Mahabubnagar?+

We are based in Mahabubnagar and work with businesses in nearby areas. For suitable projects, we can also discuss working remotely with clients elsewhere.

Do I need to provide photos and videos?+

It depends on the package and project. We can discuss shoot requirements, available footage and any location or travel costs before confirming the scope.

Is the advertising budget included?+

No. Advertising spend paid to platforms such as Meta or Google is separate from our management fee. The budget is agreed with you before any campaign goes live.

Can I request a custom package?+

Yes. If your business needs a different mix of reels, posts, website work or advertising, contact us and we'll prepare a scope around your requirements.

How do we get started?+

Send an enquiry with your business name and what you need. We'll discuss your goals, recommend a suitable scope and share the next steps.
